Welcome to My Site

This course serves as an introduction to core Computer Science concepts, giving students the groundwork needed for more advanced coursework in the field. It offers a broad overview of the discipline by exploring key areas that shape how computer systems function and what they're capable of — including computer architecture, operating systems and networking, software and the software development process, methods for organizing and visualizing data, and the broader possibilities of computing technology.
